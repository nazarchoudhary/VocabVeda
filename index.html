THE ART OF LEARNING

Word Wisdom

Learning a word is only the beginning. Understanding it, remembering it and using it naturally is what makes it yours.

01

Learn in Context

Read the definition and notice how a word is used in a sentence. Context helps you understand its meaning more clearly.

02

Make It Personal

Create your own sentence using a new word. Connecting a word with your experiences makes it easier to recall.

03

Revise Regularly

Revisit your saved vocabulary and test your memory. Short, regular practice sessions can help you retain what you learn.
